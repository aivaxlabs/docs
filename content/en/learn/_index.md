## How Learn works

Each module groups a handful of short units. A unit explains one idea with diagrams, step-by-step animations, charts and small interactive demos, then ends with a knowledge check. Units take between eight and fifteen minutes.

Your progress is stored in this browser only. Mark a unit as complete at the end of the page, or move to the next unit and it is marked for you. Nothing is sent to a server.

Learn is about concepts, not product screens. When a unit mentions something you can do on AIVAX today, it links to the matching page in the [Documentation](../docs/).
